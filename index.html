About Me

I'm Julian Vara — a computer science student with a passion for artificial intelligence, deep learning, and data-driven innovation. I began my academic journey at Riverside City College, earning an Associate of Science in Math and Science, and I'm now pursuing a B.S. in Computer Science with a Minor in Data Science at California State University, San Bernardino (GPA: 3.91).

Currently, I serve as an Undergraduate Data Science Researcher at Purdue University's Data Mine, where I'm developing a GPU-accelerated license plate recognition system using Python, OpenCV, TensorFlow, and NVIDIA CUDA. The system is capable of processing 3,000 vehicles per hour per lane, and I work closely with V2X engineers to optimize detection reliability across varied conditions.

My research experience includes multiple summer positions at CSUSB. Most recently, I worked on variable selection in penalized regression models, reducing 17,000+ predictors down to 20 while improving MSE from 0.3255 to 0.2299. I've also developed prediction models for NASA exoplanet data, achieving 99% R-squared accuracy through log transformation techniques.

Whether analyzing race data or kart racing on weekends, I thrive on continuous improvement and technical mastery.

Beyond research, I've contributed to Stalk.AI, a stock prediction platform analyzing 100+ stocks daily with machine learning. I'm also a Student Assistant Peer Advisor at CSUSB, helping diverse student populations navigate their academic journeys.

I'm always eager to collaborate on innovative projects, discuss AI ethics, or simply connect with fellow tech enthusiasts. Feel free to reach out!
